# Vertical Templates

Researched starting points for ICP criteria across common verticals. Each template maps to a `POST /api/icps` request body (or a `configure.mjs generate` entry) using the v2 `criteria` shape: `roles`, `industries`, `signals`, `companySizeRanges`.

<Callout type="warn">
The retired v1 config format supported per-vertical scoring-weight overrides (`roleWeight`, `industryWeight`, `signalWeight`, `companySizeWeight`) that tuned how heavily each criterion counted toward ICP fit for a given profile. The v2 `icp_fit` scorer has no such per-criterion weighting -- it scores each present criterion (roles, industries, signals, company size, location, niche keywords, min connections) as an unweighted average of matched-vs-checked. The only weight you can tune in v2 is the single global `icp_fit` weight in the 9-dimension composite score (see [Scoring Pipeline](/docs/claude-agent/scoring-pipeline)). The vertical-specific weight recommendations from v1 do not carry forward -- what does carry forward is which roles/industries/signals to include.
</Callout>

## Cross-Vertical Patterns

### Signal Categories

Buying signals on LinkedIn fall into five universal categories:

| Signal Category | Where Found | Examples |
|----------------|-------------|---------|
| **Pain signals** | Headline, About | "struggling with", "looking to improve", "need help with" |
| **Initiative signals** | About, Experience | "launching", "implementing", "migrating to", "evaluating" |
| **Growth signals** | Headline, About | "scaling", "expanding", "growing team", "series A/B/C" |
| **Role-change signals** | Experience dates | New role in last 90 days = budget cycle reset |
| **Technology signals** | Skills, About | Specific platforms, tools, or methodologies |

### Universal Behavioral Indicators

These LinkedIn profile patterns indicate high engagement regardless of industry:

- **Pipe-separated headlines** ("CEO | Speaker | Advisor") -- socially savvy
- **"Helping" language** in headline -- sales-minded, open to conversations
- **500+ connections** -- networkers who accept outreach
- **Creator mode enabled** -- active on platform, responsive to DMs
- **Multiple group memberships** -- community-oriented

### Role Hierarchy by Deal Size

| Deal Size | Buyer (Budget) | Influencer (Recommends) | Implementer (Uses) |
|-----------|---------------|------------------------|-------------------|
| < $10K | Director, VP | Manager, Lead | IC, Analyst |
| $10K-$100K | VP, SVP, C-suite | Director, Sr. Manager | Manager, Lead |
| $100K-$500K | C-suite, President | VP, SVP | Director, Manager |
| > $500K | CEO, Board, CFO | C-suite, SVP | VP, Director |

Put all of these roles into a single `criteria.roles` array (v2 has no separate high/medium/low priority tiers) -- the scoring engine treats role match as present/absent per contact, not weighted by seniority.

## SaaS Sales Rep

An Account Executive or SDR selling a horizontal B2B SaaS product to mid-market and enterprise buyers.

**Key roles:** CIO, CTO, VP IT, Director IT, Head of RevOps

**Key industries:** saas, software, enterprise-it

**Key signals:** digital transformation, cloud migration, evaluating solutions, RFP, vendor selection, tech stack consolidation

**Key niches:** enterprise-it, revops, mid-market-tech

## Executive Recruiter

A retained or contingency recruiter placing C-suite and VP-level candidates. Dual ICP: candidate sourcing and client development.

**Key roles:** CEO, CHRO, CPO, board member, executive coach

**Key industries:** varies by client vertical

**Key signals:** hiring, growing team, scaling, open to opportunities, succession planning

**Key niches:** hr-leaders, c-suite-tech, c-suite-finance, pe-vc, open-to-work

## Management Consultant

A partner or senior consultant selling strategy, operations, or digital transformation advisory.

**Key roles:** CEO, COO, Chief Strategy Officer, CIO, CDO, Chief Data Officer

**Key industries:** varies -- typically enterprise/mid-market across sectors

**Key signals:** transformation, restructuring, merger, operating model, strategic review, cost optimization

**Key niches:** c-suite-strategy, digital-leaders, m-and-a

## Cybersecurity / vCISO

A virtual CISO or managed security provider targeting mid-market companies without a full-time CISO.

**Key roles:** CISO, CIO, CTO, CEO, Founder (for no-CISO companies)

**Key industries:** fintech, healthtech, saas -- regulated or compliance-sensitive sectors

**Key signals:** compliance, SOC 2, ISO 27001, NIST, zero trust, incident response, cyber insurance

**Key niches:** ciso-community, startup-cto, infosec

## Financial Advisor

A wealth manager prospecting for HNW individuals, business owners approaching exits, and Centers of Influence (estate attorneys, CPAs).

**Key roles:** CEO, Founder, Owner, Estate Attorney, CPA, Business Broker

**Key industries:** varies -- prospecting is role/event-driven more than industry-driven

**Key signals:** exited, acquired, sold my company, liquidity event, retirement, angel investor, succession planning

**Key niches:** business-owners, estate-attorneys, cpa-tax, pre-exit

## Marketing Agency Owner

A 50-200 person marketing agency selling retainer services to B2C and B2B brands.

**Key roles:** CMO, VP Marketing, Head of Growth, DTC/Ecommerce leader

**Key industries:** consumer goods, ecommerce, saas

**Key signals:** agency search, RFP, rebranding, product launch, customer acquisition, agency of record

**Key niches:** cmo-brand, dtc-ecommerce, cpg-brands

## LinkedIn Profile Signal Taxonomy

Signals that predict engagement and openness to outreach, organized by detection method:

### Headline Patterns
- Pipe-separated roles: `CEO | Advisor | Speaker`
- Helping language: `Helping [persona] achieve [outcome]`
- Credentials listed: `MBA | PMP | AWS Certified`

### About Section Signals
- Connector language: "helping", "building bridges", "connecting"
- Speaker/author: "keynote", "published", "podcast"
- Builder language: "building", "scaling", "grew from X to Y"

### Experience Signals
- Recent role change (< 90 days): budget cycle reset
- Multi-company tenure: broad industry knowledge
- Board/advisory roles: network multiplier

### Connection Signals
- 500+: active networker, accepts outreach
- Creator mode: responsive to DMs
- High mutual count: warm relationship potential

## Example: Assembling a Vertical Template

```bash
node scripts/configure.mjs generate --json '{
  "profiles": {
    "vciso": {
      "name": "vCISO Services",
      "description": "Virtual CISO for mid-market companies without a full-time security lead",
      "criteria": {
        "roles": ["CISO", "CIO", "CTO", "CEO", "Founder"],
        "industries": ["fintech", "healthtech", "saas"],
        "signals": ["compliance", "SOC 2", "ISO 27001", "NIST", "zero trust", "incident response", "cyber insurance"],
        "companySizeRanges": ["51-200", "201-500"]
      }
    }
  },
  "niches": {
    "infosec": { "name": "InfoSec", "keywords": ["CISO", "security", "compliance"] }
  }
}'
```
